Utah Symphony | Utah Opera  —  Archive & Digital Assets Portal  |  Quick Reference
Portal URL: https://archive.myusuo.org  or use the USUO Archive bookmark in Chrome under the USUO folder
STEP 1 — Sign In with Your Microsoft 365 Account
1. Open Chrome and go to https://archive.myusuo.org or click the USUO Archive bookmark
2. Click the blue Sign in with Microsoft button
3. Enter your USUO email (e.g. jsmith@usuo.org) → click Next → enter your password → Sign in
4. Complete MFA if prompted (approve on your phone as usual)
5. First time only: click Grant Consent when asked — you will not see this again
Note: Use the same email and password you use for Outlook and Teams — no new account needed.
STEP 2 — The Portal Home Page
After signing in you will see five tiles — one for each archive section:
🎭 PS ArchiveFormerly Q:\PSDepShare\{Dept}\Archive
🎵 AH ArchiveFormerly T:\AHDepShare\{Dept}\Archive
📂 ProdSharedFormerly P: drive (pre-2012 files)
📂 Share ArchiveFormerly S: drive (pre-2012 files)
🎨 Digital AssetsRead/write — upload, download, overwrite
Click any tile to browse that section. A global search bar appears at the top of every page.
STEP 3 — Search Across All Archives
1. Click the search bar at the top of any page and type part of a file name
2. Results appear instantly — showing the file name, which archive it's in, and the folder path
3. Click any result to navigate directly to the folder containing that file
Note: Search covers all 556,000+ files across all five sections simultaneously. Press Escape to close results.
STEP 4 — Browse Folders & Download Files
1. Click a tile or a search result to open a folder
2. Click any subfolder to navigate deeper — use the breadcrumb trail at the top to go back
3. Click a file row or the ⬇ Download button — the file saves directly, no zip
4. Click 🏠 Archives in the breadcrumb at any time to return to the home page
Note: Archive files are read-only — you can view and download but cannot edit, delete, or upload.
STEP 5 — Digital Assets (Upload & Overwrite)
1. Click the 🎨 Digital Assets tile on the home page
2. Navigate to the folder where you want to upload
3. Click the green upload zone at the top, or drag files from Windows Explorer onto it
4. A progress bar shows upload status — the folder refreshes automatically when complete
⚠ Warning: Uploading a file with the same name as an existing file will overwrite it permanently with no undo.
Signing Out Click your name or initials in the top right corner → click Sign out
